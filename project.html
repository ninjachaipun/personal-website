Deal Overview

A Long-Hold Value-Add Acquisition

The model underwrites the acquisition of an 82-unit multi-family property at a total project cost of $25.0M ($23.5M purchase price plus closing costs and renovations), financed with $18.04M of debt (72% LTV) at a 6.0% interest rate amortized over 20 years, and $7.0M of equity from a syndicate of investors.

Rents grow at 2.5% per year (in line with Bank of Canada inflation expectations), and the property is underwritten to a 20-year hold with a 3.5% exit capitalization rate, reflecting a core market with recently improved facilities.

Investor Structure

4-Tier GP/LP Equity Waterfall

1
Preferred Return
100% of distributions to investors until a 12% preferred return is met.
2
Second Tier
Distributions continue to investors until an 11.8% IRR / 1.5x cash multiple hurdle is reached.
3
GP Catch-Up
100% of distributions to the manager until the promote catch-up is satisfied.
4
Final Split
Remaining cash flow split 70% to investors / 30% to the manager.
Methodology

How the Model Is Built

Unit & Revenue Assumptions

Unit-level rent roll by floor plan (beds/baths/dens/sq. ft.), monthly lease-up and vacancy schedules, and utilities/repairs allowances, benchmarked against a comparable Toronto property.

Debt & Cash Flow

Full monthly debt amortization schedule, operating expense assumptions (property tax, insurance, management fee, capex), and monthly-to-annual NOI and cash flow roll-ups.

Returns & Risk

Unlevered/levered IRR, NPV, and equity multiple calculated at the deal and individual-investor level, with two-way sensitivity and scenario analysis on rent growth and exit cap rate.
