Real Estate Underwriting

Projects

Real acquisition models built from public property listings — each one taken from listing to a full underwriting: rent rolls, financing, and stress-tested returns. This page grows as I take on new deals.

ⓘ How I build these: I use Claude AI throughout the pre-modeling stage of every project — researching the market, sanity-checking assumptions, and standardizing how I read a deal before it ever reaches Excel. Two purpose-built Claude skills support this: cre-brochure-screener turns a sale brochure into a structured investment memo, and unit-breakdown turns property documents into a rent-roll-ready workbook with researched comparable market rents. I don't yet have access to full offering memorandums, so these are built from public sale brochures — with room to grow toward reading full OMs and researching independently.
