Motivation

Why I'm Interested in Real Estate

An Everyday, High-Stakes Asset

Real estate is an integral part of our everyday lives — real estate prices and markets contribute heavily to household income, welfare, and investment opportunities. It acts as a vessel for investment, passive income, and most importantly a place to create value, whether it be a home to hardworking individuals or productive businesses.

Tangibility

I find real estate more interesting than other investment classes because of its tangibleness — I like being able to see and measure clearly what it is I am valuing and analyzing.

An Economic Lens

Real estate is also deeply connected to the economy and its demographics. Economics being a big area of interest of mine in university, I would love to observe the real estate market through an economic lens — combining key real estate factors like zoning, future development, location amenities, and square footage with economic factors like population growth, interest rates, and household income.
